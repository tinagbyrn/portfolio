About — fig. 01

Designer by eye,
developer by habit.

I’m Christine — Tina to most people. I’m a UI/UX designer, graphic designer and web developer from Cebu, Philippines, and I like turning ideas into simple, meaningful digital things.

For five years I’ve worked where design and code meet: customizing websites for clients at Newfold Digital, then designing and building internal web apps at Lear Corporation. I also bring automation into the mix, because the best interface is sometimes the step you no longer have to do.

I’m always learning, and I’m happiest on teams where design, technology and new ideas overlap.

fig. 02 — How I work

From a question
to a shipped thing.

Listen

Talk to the people who’ll use it. Find the real problem, not just the request.

Sketch

Map the flow and wireframe it. Cheap to change, quick to test.

Design

Type, color and components in Figma — the look and the system behind it.

Build

WordPress, HTML/CSS/JS or ASP.NET, depending on what the job needs.

Improve

Watch it in use, automate the boring parts, ship the next version.
